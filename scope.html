Scope and Benefits of HCI

Where is HCI used?

🌐 Web

Easy navigation and clear forms on websites.

📱 Mobile

Touch-friendly buttons for small screens.

🥽 VR / AR

Natural movement and comfortable immersion.

🎮 Games

Clear controls and instant feedback.

🏥 Healthcare

Medical devices that avoid dangerous mistakes.

🎓 Education

Learning apps that students can use without help.

🚗 Automotive

Dashboards that do not distract the driver.

🏠 IoT

Simple apps to control smart home devices.
