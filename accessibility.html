Visual Design, Accessibility and Responsiveness

Colour scheme

Indigo (#3f51b5) as the main colour, white cards, dark text.

Typography

Arial, 16px base size, line height 1.6.

Spacing

Consistent 14-15px padding and gaps.

Try the features

Use the buttons at the top: 🌙 dark mode, ◐ high contrast, A- / A+ font size. Your choice is remembered on all pages.
