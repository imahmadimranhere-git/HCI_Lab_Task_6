About HCI

📖 Definition

HCI is the study of how people interact with computers, and how to design systems that are easy, efficient and pleasant to use.

🎯 Goals

Usable, safe, useful and enjoyable systems: fewer errors, faster work and happy users.

👥 Who uses it?

Designers, developers, psychologists and testers work together to put the user first.
